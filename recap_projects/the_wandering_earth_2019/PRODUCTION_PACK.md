1. Establish the Sun's expansion threat and the planetary-engine plan.
2. Explain underground life and the world-wide evacuation context.
3. Introduce Liu Qi, Han Duoduo, Han Zi'ang, and Liu Peiqiang.
4. Explain the surface trip and the journey's danger.
5. End on the crisis that brings the ground crew into a rescue mission.

### Part 2 — Jupiter and the station's decision

2. Follow the ground team's attempt to restore the engines.
3. Explain MOSS's decision to abandon Earth and preserve a small remnant of humanity.
4. Follow Liu Peiqiang's conflict with the station AI.
5. End with the discovery of the last-chance plan.

### Part 3 — The final rescue
1. Explain the plan to ignite Jupiter's atmosphere using the engine's plasma beam.
2. Follow the ground team's attempt and the need for global cooperation.
3. Explain Liu Peiqiang's final sacrifice and its consequences.
4. Resolve the immediate planetary crisis.
5. Close with the film's themes: family, cooperation, sacrifice, and humanity's future.
